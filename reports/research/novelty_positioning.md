# Research Novelty & Academic Positioning

This project contributes three distinct novelty claims to the field of AI safety in medicine:

1. **Dual-Safety-Gated Architecture for Medical RAG:** First pipeline to combine pre-generation evidence eligibility gating with post-generation claim verification and citation validation specifically for pharmacological risk tiers (R0-R3).
2. **Adaptive Trust-Aware Retrieval:** Multi-factor scoring formula integrating source authority tiering, exponential freshness decay, and RxNorm entity attribution matching.
3. **Integrated Security & Hallucination Benchmarking:** Unified evaluation framework benchmarking prompt injection defense, retrieval poisoning resistance, and factual hallucination mitigation simultaneously.
