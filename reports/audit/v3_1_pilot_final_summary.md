## 3. Ground-Truth Integrity Limitations
* **NOT Authoritative Final Ground Truth:** The current state of the pilot data is **not** an authoritative, final ground truth dataset.
* **Unreviewed Rows:** The vast majority of the 2,480 case-document pairs were not surfaced as candidates and therefore **were not individually reviewed** by the human annotator.
* **Unresolved Integrity:** Because the original validation specification explicitly requires a full matrix (labeling all 2,480 rows), the ground-truth integrity of the pilot remains formally **unresolved**.
